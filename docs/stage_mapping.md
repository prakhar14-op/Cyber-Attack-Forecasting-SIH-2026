# Stage mapping (CIC-IDS-2018 attacks → 7 kill-chain stages)

The single source of truth for attack facts is [`data/attack_timeline.yaml`](../data/attack_timeline.yaml)
(UNB Table 2, verified 2026-09-05): attacker/victim addresses (internal testbed +
NAT'd public forms) and start/end times per attack. `data/timeline_labels.py` turns
that table into per-window stage labels — a window is labelled with an attack stage
only if the attacking/victim host actually **transmits** inside it (an existing
`(host, window)` row means the host was an active source), never smeared across the
day. Timeline times are local ADT; captures are UTC; the measured **+4h** offset is
applied before intersecting (see `data/attack_timeline.yaml`).

## Modeling implications (read before M4/M5) — two hard facts

**1. Severe class imbalance.** Attacks are **46,338 / 5,259,975 = 0.88%** of host-windows.
This is why the operating point is chosen from an FPR budget (0.1% / 1% of host-windows),
not a fixed threshold, and why losses are class-weighted (config). Accuracy is meaningless
here; F1@FPR and lead time are the metrics.

**2. The splits are attack-FAMILY-disjoint** — an unavoidable consequence of 4 attack days
under the day-wise anti-leakage rule (whole episodes stay in one split). No stage appears
in both train and any eval split:
- train carries `initial_access` + `impact`; val/test carry only `c2`.

Consequences, handled honestly rather than hidden:
- **Binary infiltration-probability forecast is the primary target and IS cross-family
  evaluable.** Training on bruteforce+DoS and detecting bot/infiltration C2 early on val/test
  is exactly the generalization the world model must show over a flow classifier — this is a
  feature of the split, not a defect, and it is the headline lead-time result.
- **Multi-class stage classification is limited by construction**: `c2` has no training
  signal, so the stage head cannot be expected to emit it on val/test. Per-stage metrics are
  reported only where the stage is present in both train and the eval split, with explicit
  N/A elsewhere (`benchmark_protocol.md`, M4). The world model's stage head is trained where
  labels exist and its stage output on unseen-stage families is reported as an honest
  limitation.
- M4.6's leave-one-attack-family-out mode is therefore close to the main split already; it
  will hold out a *sub*-family (e.g. Hulk vs SlowHTTPTest) where finer splits exist.

The M11 lab capture is the only path to `recon`/`lateral_movement`/`exfiltration` training
signal and to any stage appearing in more than one split.
